Printing & page access

Cut and fold (default): print A4 portrait, single-sided, one page per sheet, at 100% / actual size. Turn off “Print on both sides”. The front is above an upside-down back on the same sheet.

Use the dashed side guides to crease halfway down the artwork, then cut at the outer crop marks to make a 98 × 240 mm strip. Fold at the crease with the printed sides facing out. Do not cut across the fold. The finished badge is 98 × 120 mm.

The alternative A4 duplex layout requires double-sided printing, flipping on the long edge, at actual size. The individual printer artwork option supplies separate front and back pages with 3 mm bleed.

The PDF uses high-quality 300 dpi RGB artwork. For a commercial print run, confirm the printer accepts RGB PDFs and 3 mm bleed.
